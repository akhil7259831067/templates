How do I request a replacement debit card?
Lock the card and report it in the mobile app under Manage Cards → Report Lost or Stolen. A replacement debit card arrives in 2–5 business days; expedited shipping is available for a fee.
%%%
How long after opening an account will I receive my debit card?
After approval, your debit card is mailed within 5–7 business days.
%%%
Where can I request an additional credit card?
Log in to the mobile app and go to Cards → Request Additional Card, complete the form, and submit.
%%%
How do I activate my new credit card?
Activate instantly in the mobile app or by calling the number on the card sticker.
%%%
How do I change my card PIN?
Change your PIN at an ATM or in the mobile app under Manage PIN.
%%%
Why was my card declined?
Declines may be caused by exceeding daily limits, entering an incorrect PIN, insufficient funds, or suspected fraud.
%%%
How can I avoid card declines while traveling internationally?
Add a travel notice under Card Settings before you travel. Foreign transactions may incur a 3% fee unless your card benefits waive it.
%%%
Do premium cards waive foreign transaction fees?
Some premium cards waive the 3% foreign transaction fee; check your card’s benefits guide.
%%%
What is the minimum opening deposit for a checking account?
Most checking accounts require a minimum opening deposit of $25 to $100.
%%%
Which features come with checking accounts?
Checking includes online bill pay, mobile check deposit, paperless statements, and access to over 15,000 fee‑free ATMs.
%%%
What do savings accounts earn and how is interest paid?
Savings accounts earn interest calculated daily and paid monthly. Rates vary by balance tier.
%%%
What documents are required to open an account?
Provide a government‑issued photo ID, Social Security Number, and proof of address. Non‑residents need valid visa and immigration documentation.
%%%
Can I open an account online and how long does it take?
Yes. Open an account online by selecting Open an Account, uploading your photo ID, and completing an e‑signature. The process takes 5–10 minutes.
%%%
How do I close my account?
Ensure all transactions are settled and the balance is zero, then request closure online or at a branch. You will receive a confirmation email.
%%%
How do I switch to paperless statements?
Enable paperless delivery in Documents & Settings. You’ll receive an email when a new monthly statement is ready.
%%%
When are monthly statements generated?
Monthly statements are generated on the last business day of each month and can be downloaded as PDFs.
%%%
How do I deposit a check on mobile and when will funds be available?
Use Deposit Check in the mobile app to photograph the front and back. Funds are usually available the next business day; the first $225 is available immediately and the remainder clears within two business days.
%%%
What is overdraft protection and how do I enable it?
Link a savings account or credit card to automatically cover overdrafts. A small transfer fee may apply.
%%%
How do I pay bills online?
Use Bill Pay to add a payee and schedule one‑time or recurring payments in online or mobile banking.
%%%
Can I transfer money to another bank and how long does it take?
Yes. Link your external account; transfers complete in 1–3 business days after micro‑deposit verification.
%%%
Are Zelle transfers instant?
Eligible Zelle transfers between U.S. accounts typically complete within minutes.
%%%
How do I send an international wire and what are the fees?
Submit an international wire in online banking using the recipient’s SWIFT/BIC. Funds typically arrive in 2–3 business days and fees are $30–$45.
%%%
What are domestic wire requirements and timing?
Domestic wires can be sent during business hours through online banking and typically post the same business day depending on the receiving bank.
%%%
What is the ATM daily withdrawal limit?
You can withdraw up to $1,000 per day from ATMs, depending on account type.
%%%
How do I view my credit score?
View your FICO score for free in online banking; it updates monthly and does not affect your credit.
%%%
What rewards do credit cards offer?
Earn 1.5% cash back on purchases redeemable for travel, statement credits, or gift cards.
%%%
How are APRs determined for credit products?
APR varies by credit profile and product. See your cardholder or loan agreement for specific rates.
%%%
How does the bank monitor for fraud?
Transactions are monitored 24/7 for unusual activity. Suspicious activity may trigger alerts or a temporary block until confirmed.
%%%
How do I dispute an unauthorized transaction and how long does it take?
Open the transaction in the app and select Report an Issue. Investigations usually complete within 10 business days.
%%%
What should I do if I forget my password or get locked out?
Use Forgot Password to verify via SMS or email and reset. After five failed login attempts the account locks for security.
%%%
What are customer support hours and accessibility options?
Support is available 24/7 by phone or chat. Branches operate Monday–Friday 9 a.m.–5 p.m. and Saturday 9 a.m.–1 p.m. Accessibility options include screen‑reader support and large text modes.
%%%
How does the bank protect my data and how can I manage privacy preferences?
Customer data is encrypted in transit and at rest in compliance with the Gramm‑Leach‑Bliley Act. You can opt out of marketing and third‑party sharing in Privacy Settings.
%%%
Where can I find nearby branches and ATMs?
Use the Find Us feature in the mobile app to locate nearby branches and ATMs using GPS.
%%%
Are safe deposit boxes available?
Yes. Safe deposit boxes are available at select branches with annual rental fees and multiple sizes.
%%%
Does the bank offer currency exchange?
Yes. Currency exchange for major currencies is available at select branches with daily updated rates and a service fee.
